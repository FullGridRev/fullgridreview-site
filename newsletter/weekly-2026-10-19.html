Weekly digest · Off week before Austin

Antonelli's match point is six days away. Here's what moved while F1 waited.

No race this weekend, but Haas picked its next rookie, Red Bull locked in its boss, Africa got a Grand Prix, and Verstappen went GT3 racing.

The week in F1

Haas promotes Rafael Camara for 2027

The 21-year-old Brazilian Ferrari junior gets a multi-year Haas race deal, taking the seat Esteban Ocon leaves at the end of this season. Camara is leading Formula 2 in his rookie year, seven points clear of Red Bull junior Nikola Tsolov, and Ollie Bearman is expected to stay alongside him. One more Ferrari-linked driver on the grid also means one fewer obvious landing spot for anyone else hunting a 2027 seat.

Formula 1 is going back to Africa

F1 announced a 10-year deal with Rwanda on Tuesday. The race will run at a new, still-unbuilt circuit in Bugesera, south of Kigali, with the first Grand Prix targeted for the early 2030s. It would be the first world championship race in Africa since Kyalami in 1993. Don't book flights yet; a date and a finished track are both some way off.

Red Bull commits to Mekies through 2031

Laurent Mekies, team principal and CEO since replacing Christian Horner in mid-2025, signed a five-year extension. With Verstappen winning the last two races, Red Bull clearly likes the direction, though the next item could make 2027 harder.

Red Bull's sidepod trick looks set to go in 2027

Red Bull has used a cable along the sidepod ridge, a "floor body stay," to qualify for a much tighter edge radius than the bodywork rules normally allow, giving it sharper aero edges since Austria. Reports this week say the FIA will shrink the allowed dimension from 20mm to 5mm next year, which would force a redesign. That comes from reporting rather than a published FIA statement, so treat the detail as likely, not final.

Paddock notes

Verstappen's GT3 debut: ninth from 24th. Racing the #3 Verstappen Racing Mercedes-AMG at the Portimão GT World Challenge Europe finale, he was reprimanded after a practice collision, and in the race his team took a drive-through after teammate Chris Lulham spun another Mercedes. Verstappen rejoined 24th and charged to ninth in the closing stint. A GetSpeed Mercedes won.

Qatar and Abu Dhabi: still on, still watched. Teams said this week they need a final answer on the last two rounds by mid-October because of the war in Iran, with Imola kept as a fallback. Stefano Domenicali's line since early October is that both races will run as planned, and nothing official has changed that.

Beganovic lined up as Ferrari reserve for 2027. The Swedish F2 driver's promotion, reported last week, now looks set to carry into next season.

Alonso in the desert. Fernando Alonso was spotted testing a Prodrive Dakar car in Morocco, fueling talk of another Dakar Rally run. Nothing has been announced.

To be champion on Sunday night, Antonelli has to win the race. That takes him to 365. Russell then needs to finish ninth or lower and Hamilton fifth or lower. If Russell is exactly ninth, the lead is 125, matching the points left; Antonelli would still win any tie on count-back with eight wins to Russell's three. Any finish below P1 for Antonelli sends the fight to Mexico City. One wildcard: reports say Mercedes may need fresh power unit parts in Austin, and a grid penalty for either driver would reshape the odds.
